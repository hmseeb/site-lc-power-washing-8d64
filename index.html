FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What areas do you serve?

We serve Los Angeles and the surrounding communities. If you're just outside the city, send your address in the quote form and we'll confirm whether it's within our service range.

What's the difference between pressure washing and soft washing?

Pressure washing uses high-pressure water and is ideal for hard surfaces like concrete, brick and pavers. Soft washing uses low pressure with cleaning solutions and is the safe choice for stucco, siding, painted surfaces and roofs. We choose the method based on the surface so nothing gets damaged.

How much does a job cost?

Pricing depends on the square footage, surface type and how heavy the build-up is. Estimates are free — send us the details and we'll give you a clear price before any work begins.

How often should I have my property washed?

Most Los Angeles homes benefit from a yearly exterior wash, while driveways and high-traffic walkways often look best with cleaning every 6 to 12 months. Storefronts and commercial sidewalks usually need a more frequent schedule.

Do you need access to water and power?

In most cases we use an outdoor spigot on site. Let us know in your request if water access is limited and we'll plan accordingly.

Will pressure washing damage my paint, stucco or plants?

Not when it's done properly. We reduce pressure for delicate surfaces, pre-rinse and protect landscaping, and use surface-appropriate methods throughout the job.
